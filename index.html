📜 System Architecture & Facial Recognition Workflow

Technical architecture of the face scanning attendance system.

1. Operational Workflow

1. Session Initialization: Teacher begins a 1 hr 15 min class period. The system activates the scanning window and starts the countdown.
2. Facial Biometric Scanning: Students scan their face at the terminal.
3. Anti-Proxy Detection: The system validates live facial landmarks and matches against the enrolled student face vector with >98% confidence.
4. Duplicate Prevention: If a student scans multiple times in the same session, the system alerts: "Face Already Scanned" and rejects the duplicate.
5. Automated Present/Absent Classification: Clicking Finalize & Submit immediately categorizes enrolled students into Present and Absent rosters.
6. Report Export: An official CSV/Excel attendance sheet is generated for department records.
